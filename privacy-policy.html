Privacy Policy

How CleanWave KC collects, uses and protects your information.

Effective date: [Month Day, Year] · CleanWave KC, Olathe, Kansas

This Privacy Policy explains how CleanWave KC ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information you provide through our website, forms, phone calls, text messages and email.

Information we collect

  • Contact details such as your name, company name, role, email address and phone number.
  • Facility details you share, such as address, facility type, square footage, cleaning frequency and special requirements.
  • Website usage data such as pages visited, referring source and campaign parameters, collected through cookies and similar tools.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your quote request and schedule walkthroughs.
  • To send appointment confirmations, reminders and follow-up about your quote.
  • To provide and improve our services and measure our advertising.

SMS / text messaging

If you opt in, we will send text messages about your quote, appointments and service. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, and reply HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business (such as our CRM, scheduling and email providers) under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. Facility access details such as keys and alarm codes are shared only with your assigned cleaning team.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails using the link in any message, opt out of texts by replying STOP, or ask us to update or delete your information by contacting us.
